# Benchmark verb frequency and irregularity

Generated by `research/scripts/score_benchmark_verbs.py`.

Zipf-lemma scores are the log10 of per-billion frequency, summed over a canonical inflected-form set (infinitive + present indicative x6 + gerund + past participle) using `wordfreq` (Wikipedia + OpenSubtitles + news + web). Tier boundaries (high / mid / low) are the 33rd and 67th percentiles of the Zipf-lemma distribution over a full verb census per language (n=1,180 Spanish verbs from wordfreq top-30k, validated by verbecc; n=2,265 English). Verbs outside the census are scored with the same Zipf function and compared against the frozen boundaries. Irregularity is tense-specific: `yes` means the verbecc conjugation of (verb, tense, person, number) differs from the regular-paradigm form.
