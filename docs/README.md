## Conventions

- **Experiment reports** (`experiment-results/`) — reproducible runs with stated parameters and analysis; suitable for thesis evidence.
- **Spike results** (`spike-results/`) — machine-readable output; regenerated by scripts under `research/prototyping/`.
- **Spike reports** (`spike-reports/`) — human interpretation of spike data; may be informal.
- **Plans** (`plans/`) — living design docs; may lag implementation.
